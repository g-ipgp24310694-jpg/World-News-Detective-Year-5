# World News Detective — Your Voice Recording Guide

This is a **draft framework**. Rewrite and personalise the scripts, and record your own narration for your academic showcase.

## What to record

Record **three separate audio files** (the welcome is optional). Do not record the square-bracket instructions — they are speaking cues only.

### 1. Welcome Mission (optional)
**Voice:** Excited, cheerful, warm. **Pause:** 1 second after each sentence.

Hello, young detectives! [pause]

Welcome to World News Detective! [pause]

Today, we are travelling to two different countries to discover some amazing school stories. [pause]

Listen carefully, collect the clues, and solve the news mysteries! [pause]

Are you ready? [pause]

Let's begin our adventure!

### 2. Report A — Sarawak, Malaysia
**Voice:** Friendly young newsreader. **Pause:** 1–1.5 seconds after each sentence.

Good morning, young detectives! [pause]

Welcome to Young Voices News. [pause]

Our first story comes from Sarawak, Malaysia. [pause]

On **Thursday**, Year Five pupils at **Ria Primary School** held a **Culture Sharing Day** in the **school hall**. [pause]

Families brought **photographs** and taught **traditional games**. [pause]

Pupils shared stories about Gawai, Hari Raya, and Chinese New Year. [pause]

After the event, pupils made **thank-you cards in English**. [pause]

Their teacher, Miss Lim, said, "We learn when we listen to each other." [pause]

That is our first news report! [pause]

Did you hear all the important clues?

### 3. Report B — Wellington, New Zealand
**Voice:** Friendly, curious young newsreader. **Pause:** 1–1.5 seconds after each sentence.

Hello again, detectives! [pause]

This is Young Voices News. [pause]

Our second story comes from **Wellington, New Zealand**. [pause]

On **Friday**, pupils at **Harbour Primary School** held a **Family Stories Day** in the **library**. [pause]

They showed **photographs**, shared **family stories**, and taught one another **greetings in different languages**. [pause]

After the event, teams made **colourful posters** about respecting other traditions. [pause]

A pupil named Ruby said, "Every family has a story worth hearing." [pause]

And that is our second news report! [pause]

Now, detectives, what do these two schools have in common?

## Method A — Record inside your website

1. Publish your website on GitHub Pages (https:// address) and open **Teacher Studio**.
2. Select **Welcome**, **Report A**, or **Report B** from the dropdown.
3. Click **Start recording**, then **Allow microphone** when the browser asks.
4. Read the relevant script slowly and clearly, then click **Stop**.
5. Press **Play** under the recording controls to hear your voice. Re-record if needed.
6. Click **Download**. Keep the filename created by the site; for example, `news-a.webm` or `news-a.m4a`.
7. Repeat separately for the other recordings. The site records locally: it does not automatically upload audio to GitHub.

If the recording button is unavailable, use Method B.

## Method B — Record with your phone or computer

1. Use your phone's voice recorder or your computer's Sound Recorder/Voice Recorder application.
2. Record each script in a quiet room, with the microphone a comfortable distance from your mouth.
3. Save three individual audio files, not one long file.
4. Export to a format supported by the website, preferably MP3 or M4A. A supported WebM recording from the website also works. Do **not** change a file's extension just to pretend to convert formats.
5. Name the files using one of these matching sets:

| Recording | MP3 | WebM | M4A |
|---|---|---|---|
| Welcome (optional) | `welcome.mp3` | `welcome.webm` | `welcome.m4a` |
| Report A | `news-a.mp3` | `news-a.webm` | `news-a.m4a` |
| Report B | `news-b.mp3` | `news-b.webm` | `news-b.m4a` |

## How to insert your voice into GitHub

1. Open your `world-news-detective` GitHub repository.
2. Click the `assets` folder, then click `audio`.
3. Select **Add file → Upload files**.
4. Upload your appropriately named audio files into that folder.
5. Select **Commit changes**.
6. Open your GitHub Pages website after the changes deploy. Reload and play the welcome and reports to test.
7. If the browser doesn't play a file, check the exact filename, extension, and folder path.

**Important:** Your audio becomes publicly accessible through the published website. Record only your own voice, not any pupil voices or identifiable pupil information.
